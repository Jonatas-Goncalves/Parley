Local-first language learning

Practice languages with your own voice.

Parley is a desktop language tutor that runs entirely on your computer. Talk with local AI, train pronunciation, listen to spoken stories, and get feedback without accounts, cloud services, or telemetry.

Linux & Windows Local AI Voice practice

Built for real practice

Parley combines local language models, speech recognition, and speech synthesis into a focused desktop experience for language learners.

🎙️

Voice Practice

Have role-play conversations with a local AI tutor across practical everyday scenarios.

🗣️

Pronunciation

Read phrases aloud, compare your speech, and practice until it sounds natural.

✍️

Complete the Sentence

Train listening and spelling by completing sentences from spoken prompts.

📚

Spoken Stories

Listen to public-domain stories with on-demand audio downloads and local playback.

🧠

Local AI Models

Use llama.cpp or Ollama, choose model tiers, and manage local GGUF models.

🔒

Private by Design

No account, cloud service, or telemetry required. Your practice stays on your machine.

See Parley in action

A clean GTK interface designed for speaking, listening, and learning.
